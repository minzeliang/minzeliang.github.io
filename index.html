Academic Foundation

Calculus, linear algebra, college physics, circuit theory, analog/digital electronics, signals and systems — plus human anatomy & physiology and biochemistry, giving me a truly interdisciplinary "medicine + engineering" background.

Hardware & Embedded

Experienced with STM32 microcontroller development, sensor applications, PCB design, soldering and debugging — able to build schematic designs and prototypes of simple medical electronic devices.
